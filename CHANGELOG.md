# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-21

First release.

- Menu bar item in four styles, with an alarm state, and a popover with charge, CPU, memory, disk,
  heat, fan modes, thirstiest apps and flavours
- Window with ten modules: Overview (Juice score, Squeeze test), CPU, Memory, Disk, Network, Battery,
  Fans, Sensors, Alerts, Flavors
- Fan control through a privileged helper: Auto, Chill, Blast and a custom temperature curve, with a
  watchdog that hands the fans back to macOS
- Alert rules with duration, hysteresis and actions (notify, blast the fans, turn the glass red)
- 24-hour history, CSV export, plain-text report, Shortcuts actions, keep-awake, launch at login
- 3D icon set modelled and rendered in Blender
